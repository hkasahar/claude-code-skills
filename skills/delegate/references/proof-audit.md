# Evidence-based review

A model report is evidence, not a proof certificate. Separate theorem truth from the validity of the submitted argument. An alternative proof may establish the result without repairing the manuscript's stated argument.

1. Freeze the exact statement, assumptions, definitions, argument, dependencies and original-source excerpts with locations. Record packet hashes and authors. Keep orchestrator guesses and other reviewers' reports out of fresh prompts.
2. Check converted text against the original page image, including signs, quantifiers, indices, convergence modes and matrix orientation. Unavailable sources remain unverified; agreement cannot authenticate them.
3. Run expressible algebraic, symbolic, small-scale numerical or formal checks first. Record assumptions, scope and certificate strength. Floating-point agreement and simplifier output are evidence. A formal checker certifies only the encoded statement and axioms; separately compare that statement with the manuscript.
4. Obtain fresh reviews of the same version. A reconstruction reviewer can see the statement and established dependencies before the proposed proof. Preserve raw reports, provenance and coverage limitations. Multiple runs can share errors; their agreement implies no error probability.
5. Require precise findings: exact step, implication required, assumptions available, evidence and justified repairs. Check quantifiers, local/global claims, support, rank, null sets, labels, boundaries, integrability, dependence, limit interchanges and uniformity as applicable.
6. Resolve every substantive concern from sources, calculations or counterexamples. One demonstrated gap remains a gap despite a majority. Read complete reports. Mark unresolved claims explicitly. Changed statements or assumptions create a new version requiring review of affected dependencies and the final result.

The wrapper expects `VERDICT: CORRECT|ERROR|GAP`. Interpret `CORRECT` as “no defect located in this review”, not formal certification. `ERROR` and `GAP` are findings. Malformed output or failed requested legs make a round incomplete. Optional Claude investigation gathers evidence; another vote alone never settles disagreement.
